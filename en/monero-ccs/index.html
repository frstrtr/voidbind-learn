The Monero CCS proposal: a timeline

September to October 2026, from the public record

In September 2026 c2pool asked the Monero Community Crowdfunding System (CCS) to fund a daemonless Monero pool. This page lists, in order and with links, the public record of that proposal and of a review of c2pool’s payout model published at the same time. Quotes are short and attributed. Times are UTC.

Timeline

  • 2026-09-19 19:25. SChernykh, the author of Monero’s P2Pool, publishes a review of c2pool’s payout code (frstrtr/c2pool master, the V37 settlement and coinbase stages): “Fairness gaps in the c2pool payout ledger”. Its summary: “Eight ways the Work Receipts owed-ledger and coinbase selection can pay the wrong miner — several of them with every participant behaving honestly.”
  • 2026-09-25 06:30. Merge request !699 opens in the CCS repository: “c2pool: a Monero pool with no operator and no daemon (mainnet milestone, 25 XMR)”.
  • 2026-09-28 17:24. frstrtr answers on the review: “Thanks for the review, will check and revert.”
  • 2026-10-02 10:32. frstrtr posts a finding-by-finding reply: “We re-checked every finding against the code and reproduced most of them.”
  • 2026-10-03. The proposal is discussed in the #monero-community channel (public log). At 20:07 frstrtr posts on the review an answer to the question SChernykh (sech1) asked there: “if a full reward must be paid out, and ‘owed’ wallets are not owed enough to cover the full reward, what do you do?”
  • 2026-10-04 09:11. SChernykh replies with numbers: “owed balances can’t be fair.” In his example, c2pool’s own allocator gives a miner with 20% of the work “0.47 XMR of each 0.6 XMR block instead of 0.12, which is 3.94× its share”. He also shows that a block paying from a window fixed 61 heights earlier gives a well-timed hopper 1.312 where paying from the block’s own window gives 1.000, in c2pool’s own pulse test.
  • 2026-10-04 11:46 and 12:18. frstrtr replies (“refuted and recreating the canonical Work Receipts design”) and explains the change: “You’re right, and your numbers leave little room to argue.” and “So the ledger goes. Each pool block pays its own window in full, in its own coinbase, from state fixed before the block.”
  • 2026-10-04 13:37. frstrtr closes merge request !699, with the comment: “Closing this. The payout model needs to change first; I’ll come back with working code and numbers.”
  • 2026-10-04. The Work Receipts paper publishes errata: Section 9 (paying from owed balances), and the parts of the paper that rely on an owed balance, are withdrawn.

Where it stands

Monero CCS: the funding proposal (MR !699) was withdrawn by its author on 2026-10-04 while the payout model is replaced by Work Receipts (Path B). No funding application is open.

The Monero lane in c2pool, in the words of the project’s README: “Experimental (v37 lane, Path B payouts; c2pool-v37-xmr, can run daemonless; not built by the release workflow): regtest, stagenet and testnet run a pool; mainnet runs only --follow-only and needs --i-understand-mainnet even for that.” And: “Mainnet pool mode is disabled in code.”
